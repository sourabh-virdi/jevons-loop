## getting_virtual_card
Issue a virtual card. A customer might say: "How do I get a virtual card?" The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.

## lost_or_stolen_card
The card is lost or stolen. A customer might say: "My card was stolen. Please freeze my card." The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.
