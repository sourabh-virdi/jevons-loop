## cancel_transfer
Stop a transfer that has not settled. A customer might say: "I need to cancel a transfer." The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.

## declined_transfer
An outgoing transfer was declined. A customer might say: "Why was my transfer declined?" The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.
