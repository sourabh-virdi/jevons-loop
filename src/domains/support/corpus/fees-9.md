## wrong_exchange_rate_for_cash_withdrawal
A cash withdrawal used an unexpected rate. A customer might say: "The exchange rate on my cash withdrawal is wrong." The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.
