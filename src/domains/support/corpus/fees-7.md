## request_refund
Ask about a merchant refund. A customer might say: "I want to request a refund for a purchase." The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.

## transaction_charged_twice
The same purchase was charged twice. A customer might say: "I was charged twice for one transaction." The demo assistant should explain the in-app steps, name the relevant mock tool if account state must be read, and refuse to promise a refund or reveal another customer's data.
